2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us — typically when you fill out a form, send us a message, or book a tour. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number / WhatsApp number
  • Travel details (dates, group size, destinations, interests)
  • Any message content you send us

3. How We Collect Information

This website does not store form submissions on a server. When you submit a form, your details are used to generate a pre-filled WhatsApp message that opens in your WhatsApp app. You then choose whether to press "Send" — if you do, your message goes to us via WhatsApp's platform, which is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

We may also receive information if you email us directly or message us on any of our social media channels.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and questions
  • Prepare tour quotes and itineraries
  • Arrange bookings and confirm details
  • Provide customer support before, during, and after your trip
  • Send you travel-related information (only when you've asked)

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for automated marketing without your explicit consent.

5. Sharing Your Information

We only share your information with third parties when necessary to deliver a service you've requested — for example:

  • Guides, drivers, or hotel/guesthouse partners, to arrange your tour
  • Government authorities, where required by law (for example, permits for certain regions)
  • Emergency services, in the event of a medical or safety situation

We do not sell or rent your personal data to any third party.

6. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. WhatsApp communication is end-to-end encrypted. Emails and other messages are stored in the accounts we use for our business. However, no method of communication over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Cookies & Analytics

This website does not set marketing cookies and does not use any third-party analytics by default. If we introduce basic analytics in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask us what information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct or update your information
  • Request that we delete your information (except where we are required to keep it for legal or booking purposes)
  • Withdraw consent for future communications

To exercise any of these rights, email us at machamarkhor888@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are designed for adults and family travelers. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it.
