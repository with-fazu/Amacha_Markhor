Trekking

Walk to Where the Roads End

Some of the world's greatest treks are here — from the classic Fairy Meadows approach to Nanga Parbat, to multi-day routes through the Karakoram, Rakaposhi's base camp, and the high meadows of Nagar.

We lead treks for all levels — from first-timers on a two-day hike to experienced trekkers on week-long high-altitude journeys. All treks are supported by local porters, guides, and experienced cooks.

  • • Fairy Meadows & Nanga Parbat Base Camp
  • • Rakaposhi Base Camp
  • • Deosai Plains crossing
  • • Custom high-altitude treks

Culture

Meet the People Behind the Mountains

Gilgit-Baltistan is home to extraordinary cultural diversity — Ismaili, Shia and Sunni communities, each with their own language, music, food and customs. On our cultural journeys, you don't just see this — you're welcomed into it.

Stay in family-run guesthouses. Share tea in village homes. Visit centuries-old forts and mosques. Taste food you won't find anywhere else.

  • • Baltit & Altit Forts (Hunza)
  • • Khaplu Palace & Shigar Fort (Baltistan)
  • • Chaqchan Mosque (Khaplu, 14th century)
  • • Village homestays & family meals
  • • Local craft, music & storytelling

Photography

Chase the Light

Some of the world's most photographed mountains are in our backyard — Rakaposhi, Passu Cones, Nanga Parbat, K2's distant face. We design photo-focused trips around the light: golden hours, dark skies for astrophotography, and quiet moments in villages.

Our guides know exactly where to be and when — no guesswork, no wasted mornings.

  • • Golden-hour viewpoints at altitude
  • • Astrophotography at Deosai & Skardu
  • • Village portraits & cultural scenes
  • • Landscape photography guidance available
