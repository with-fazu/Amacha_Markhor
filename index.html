Who We Are

Mountain Journeys, Thoughtfully Planned

Amacha Markhor Travel and Tour is a Gilgit-Baltistan-based travel company built around one belief: that this region deserves to be experienced with respect, curiosity, and care. Our journeys are designed by people who grew up here — who know the roads, the seasons, the stories, and the people behind every place.

Responsible Travel

Travel That Gives Back

We believe tourism should strengthen the places it visits. That's why we hire local guides, support small guesthouses, encourage leave-no-trace trekking, and keep group sizes small so communities and landscapes benefit from every journey.
