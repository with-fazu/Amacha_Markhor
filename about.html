Our Story

Why Amacha Markhor

Amacha Markhor Travel and Tour was founded with one simple belief — that Gilgit-Baltistan is not a destination to be "checked off", but a place to be experienced with respect, patience, and curiosity.

Our team is from this region. We grew up on these roads, drank tea in these villages, and know the valleys in every season. Every itinerary we design comes from that local knowledge — not from a template or a fixed menu.

We work with local guides, family-run guesthouses, and small businesses so that the money you spend stays in the mountains. We keep groups small, follow leave-no-trace principles, and plan each journey around the seasons and the people who call this place home.
